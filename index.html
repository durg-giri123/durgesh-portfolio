Muse EEG Spectral & Normalization Pipeline

4 Channels: AF7, AF8, TP9, TP10 • FFT & PSD Analysis
  • ▸ 138 Multi-Channel Recordings: Built automated filtering and quality verification pipelines across frontal and temporal electrode placements.
  • ▸ Spectral Band Power: Executed Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and Power Spectral Density (PSD) to extract absolute and relative power for Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, and Gamma bands.
  • ▸ Pre-Session Normalization: Quantified relative Alpha, Beta, and Gamma power across experimental cohorts: VR+Audio, Audio Only, and Control groups.
SciPy Signal FFT / PSD ANOVA Normalization
💓

EmotiBit Physiological & HRV Analysis

54,269 Raw Intervals -> 39,798 Clean Intervals
  • ▸ Physiological Gating: Implemented strict 300–2000 ms threshold filtering coupled with a 30% deviation anomaly cutoff to eliminate sensor artifacts.
  • ▸ HRV Metric Extraction: Modeled autonomic modulation by computing RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences) and SDNN (Standard Deviation of NN intervals).
  • ▸ Repeated-Measures ANOVA: Evaluated multi-session longitudinal shifts across Pre-session, Post-Session 1, and Post-Session 2 for cross-modal physiological comparison.
RMSSD / SDNN Outlier Detection Longitudinal Modeling
